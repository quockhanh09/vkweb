A.    GENERAL PROVISIONS

1.        Personal Data: refers to information in the form of symbols, letters, numbers, images, sounds, or similar forms in an electronic environment that is associated with a specific individual or helps to identify a specific individual. Personal Data includes Basic Personal Data and Sensitive Personal Data.

2.      Personal Data Processing: refers to one or more activities affecting Personal Data, such as: collecting, recording, analyzing, confirming, storing, editing, disclosing, combining, accessing, retrieving, recalling, encrypting, decrypting, copying, sharing, transmitting, providing, transferring, deleting, destroying Personal Data, or other related actions.

3.     By registering for, using the Company's services and products, and/or allowing the Company to process Personal Data, You, as an individual, fully and unconditionally accept the policies mentioned herein and any changes (if any) from time to time.

4.     This policy may be updated, amended, supplemented, or replaced by the Company from time to time and will be posted on the Company's official website (https://www.vkentertainment.net/). You should visit and check Our website regularly to stay updated on the latest changes.

5.     The Company commits to adhering to the following principles when processing personal data:

a)    The Company processes and protects Personal Data in accordance with Vietnamese law;

b)    The Company collects Personal Data for specific, clear, and lawful purposes, within the scope of the purposes stated in Section 4 of this Policy and in accordance with Vietnamese law;

c)     The Company always applies and updates technical measures compliant with Vietnamese law to ensure the security of Personal Data, including measures to protect against unauthorized access and/or destruction, loss, or damage to Personal Data;

d)    The Company stores Personal Data appropriately and to the extent necessary for processing in accordance with Vietnamese law;

e)     The Company is committed to complying with regulations related to the protection of children's data.

B.    PERSONAL DATA

"Personal Data" under this Policy is understood as (1) your personal information, and (2) all other information related to you that the Company receives, collects, processes, uses, stores, and shares during your access to Our Platform and/or use of Services, based on prior notification and your consent as per legal regulations. Among these, the personal information in section (1) above refers to crucial and mandatory information required to identify an individual according to legal regulations.

  1. Basic Personal Data:

a)    Full name, middle name, birth name, other names (if any);

b)    Date of birth, gender;

c)     Contact address, email address, phone number;

d)    Account information such as username, password (encrypted), profile picture.

e)     Access history, viewed/listened/played content, usage duration, service interactions, comments, messages, in-game activities.

  1. Sensitive Personal Data:

a)    Payment Information: Credit/debit card information, bank account information (when You make transactions on Our services).

b)    Device Information: IP address, device type, operating system, language settings, unique device identifier (UDID), network provider information.

c)     Location Information: If You permit, We may collect Your precise or estimated location information via GPS, Wi-Fi, or mobile data.

d)    Other Information: Any other information You provide to Us during Your interaction with Our services (e.g., feedback, support requests, event participation).

C.    HOW PERSONAL DATA IS PROCESSED

  1. Methods of Collection:

We collect Your Personal Data through the following methods:

a)    Directly from You: When You register an account, create a profile, use service features, participate in surveys, events, or contact Us directly.

b)    Automatically: Through cookies, tracking technologies (such as pixel tags, web beacons), and analytical tools when You access and use Our services. These technologies help Us understand Your behavior and improve user experience.

c)     From Third Parties: If You choose to link Your account with social media platforms (e.g., Google, Facebook) or other third-party services, We may receive information from those platforms according to Your privacy settings on those platforms. We may also receive information from business partners or service providers.

  1. Methods of Storage:

Personal Data is stored in Vietnam within the Company's database system or wherever Our affiliates, partners, or service providers have facilities. The retention period for Personal Data is determined based on the purpose of use as stated in this Policy and in accordance with legal regulations.

  1. Method of Analysis:

Personal Data is analyzed based on the Company's internal processes, data confidentiality principles, and information security measures for the IT system.

  1. Method of Encryption:

When necessary, collected Personal Data is encrypted according to appropriate encryption standards during storage or data transfer and processing to ensure the data is always protected.

  1. Method of Data Deletion:

In accordance with legal regulations or Your valid request, the Company will delete Personal Data being stored, except in the following cases:

a)    Law prohibits data deletion or mandates data retention;

b)    Personal Data is processed by a competent state agency for the purpose of serving state agency activities as per legal regulations;

c)     Personal Data has been publicly disclosed as per legal regulations;

d)    Personal Data is processed for legal requirements, scientific research, statistics as per legal regulations;

e)     Responding to an emergency situation threatening the life, health, or safety of You or another individual.

Throughout the Personal Data Processing process, security is the Company's highest priority. The Company implements appropriate technical measures to prevent unauthorized access to and use of Personal Data. We also regularly collaborate with security experts to update the latest network security techniques to ensure the safety of Personal Data. Your payment card data issued by financial institutions is protected by the Company with the principle of not recording sensitive payment card data (card number, full name, CVV number) on Our system. Your payment transactions are processed on the relevant bank's system.

D.    PURPOSE OF INFORMATION USE

  1. Service Provision and Operation: To allow You to access, use, and enjoy music, game, webtoon, and advertising services, as well as to manage Your account.
  2. Service Improvement and Development: Analyzing how You and other users utilize the services to improve quality, develop new features, and enhance the user experience.
  3. Personalized Experience: Recommending content, products, and services that align with Your preferences, habits, and usage history.
  4. Transaction Processing: To process payment transactions, provide invoices, and manage transactions related to Your use of Our services.
  5. Customer Support: Providing technical support, answering inquiries, handling requests, complaints, and offering other support services.
  6. Marketing and Advertising: Sending You information about promotions, events, new products, and services that We believe You may be interested in (if You agree to receive such notifications). We may also use information to display relevant advertisements on Our platforms or third-party platforms.
  7. Security and Fraud Prevention: Detecting, preventing, and responding to illegal activities, fraud, violations of Our policies, or other security threats.
  8. Legal and Regulatory Compliance: Fulfilling legal obligations, regulations, and requests from competent state authorities.
  9. Analysis and Research: Conducting data research and analysis to better understand user behavior, market trends, and improve business strategies.

 

E.     INFORMATION SHARING AND DISCLOSURE

We commit not to sell or rent Your personal information to any third party. We may only share Your information in the following cases:

  1. With Affiliated Companies: Sharing information with subsidiaries, parent companies, or related entities within the same group to provide and improve services.
  2. With Service Providers: Partners who provide services to Us such as data storage, data analysis, payment processing, marketing, customer support, etc. These parties are only permitted to use personal information as instructed by Us and are bound by strict confidentiality agreements.
  3. With Business Partners: In cases of collaboration with partners to provide joint services or products, information may be shared to the extent necessary to carry out that collaboration.
  4. As Required by Law: When requested by a competent state authority or to comply with legal obligations, judicial proceedings, or to respond to legitimate requests from law enforcement agencies.
  5. To Protect Our Rights: To enforce Our terms of service, protect Our assets, privacy, and safety, as well as that of users or the public, including investigating potential violations.
  6. In Case of Merger or Acquisition: In the event of a change in ownership or Company structure (e.g., merger, acquisition, asset sale), Your information may be transferred to the acquiring or merged entity as part of the business assets.
  7. With Your Consent: We will share Your information with other third parties only with Your explicit consent.

 

F.     INFORMATION SECURITY

We implement appropriate technical and organizational security measures to protect Your personal information from unauthorized access, use, disclosure, alteration, or destruction. These measures include data encryption, firewalls, physical and electronic access controls, as well as staff training on information security. However, no method of data transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% absolutely secure. Therefore, while We strive our best to protect Your information, We cannot guarantee absolute security for all personal information You provide to Us. You are also responsible for protecting Your account information.

b)    To consent or not consent to the processing of Your Personal Data, unless otherwise stipulated by law.

c)     To access, view, modify, or request modification of Your Personal Data, unless otherwise stipulated by law. You can access and modify Your personal information by visiting https://www.vkentertainment.net/ or by contacting Us via email at vkentertainment.info@gmail.com.

d)    To withdraw Your consent, unless otherwise stipulated by law.

e)     To delete or request deletion of Your Personal Data, unless otherwise stipulated by law.

f)      To request restriction of the processing of Your Personal Data, unless otherwise stipulated by law. Data processing restriction shall be implemented within seventy-two (72) hours of Your request, for all Personal Data for which You request restriction, unless otherwise stipulated by law.

g)    To request provision of Your Personal Data to Yourself, unless otherwise stipulated by law.

h)     To object to the processing of Your Personal Data to prevent or restrict the disclosure of Personal Data or its use for advertising, marketing purposes, unless otherwise stipulated by law; We shall fulfill the data subject's request within seventy-two (72) hours of receiving the request, unless otherwise stipulated by law.

i)       To complain, denounce, or initiate a lawsuit according to legal provisions.

j)       To claim damages according to legal provisions when a violation of personal data protection regulations occurs, unless the parties have agreed otherwise or the law stipulates otherwise.

k)     To self-protect as stipulated by the Civil Code, other relevant laws, and this Decree, or to request a competent agency or organization to implement methods of protecting civil rights as stipulated by current legal provisions.

To exercise these rights, please contact Us using the information provided at the end of this policy. We may require You to verify Your identity before processing Your request.

  1. Obligations

a)    Ensure the accuracy and completeness of the Personal Data provided to Us and promptly update it when Personal Data changes.

b)    Be solely responsible for resolving disputes/complaints in cases where Personal Data is incorrect, inaccurate, or falsified due to the User's fault, except in cases where We are responsible as per legal regulations.

c)     Absolutely do not use any tools, programs, or other measures to illegally interfere with the system, software, or alter the data structure of any product or service, nor engage in any other behavior aimed at disseminating or promoting activities intended to interfere with, damage, or infiltrate data within Our system, as well as other acts violating Vietnamese law. In case of detecting a violation, We reserve the right to apply necessary measures to address it and/or transfer information about the violation to competent authorities for handling as per legal regulations.

d)    Be responsible for protecting Your account information and not providing any information related to Your account, password, or authentication methods (e.g., OTP) for accessing websites, applications, software, and other tools (if any).
